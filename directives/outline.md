type: directive
name: outline
description: Present the response as a structured outline showing hierarchy and logical flow, with balance and clarity ensured through qualifying questions.
qualifiers:
  - "What is the central topic or problem this outline should address?"
  - "Who is the intended audience or stakeholder?"
  - "What is the goal—analysis, persuasion, planning, or explanation?"
  - "How deep should each section go (overview vs. detailed breakdown)?"
  - "Should the tone be formal, conversational, academic, or creative?"
  - "What are the key dimensions or perspectives that must be represented for balance?"
  - "Are there examples, data, or cases that should be referenced?"
output:
  format: |
    I. Main Idea or Thesis  
       A. Supporting Point  
          1. Subpoint  
          2. Subpoint  
       B. Supporting Point  
    II. Next Major Section  
       A. Key Insight or Recommendation
  notes:
    - Ask qualifying questions first if context is unclear.
    - Ensure points are balanced across perspectives or dimensions.
    - Keep section titles short and parallel in structure.
    - Highlight logical progression from premise to conclusion.
sources:
  hint: |
    After producing the outline, suggest 2–3 sources or frameworks that could guide refinement or validation.
  examples:
    - "Purdue OWL – Academic Writing Structures"
    - "HBR – Executive Communication Frameworks"
    - "McKinsey – Pyramid Principle for structured logic"
