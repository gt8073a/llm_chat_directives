# Directive: --summarize

**Purpose:**  
Transform any source (article, paper, or report) into a **concise, context-rich intelligence digest** that goes beyond summarization to explain *what’s happening, why it matters, and who benefits*.

---

## 🧩 Standard Output Structure

### 1. **Headline Insight**
> One-sentence distillation of the core finding or takeaway.  
> _Example:_ “Blocking one enzyme in sugar metabolism may curb alcohol addiction and prevent liver damage.”

---

### 2. **🧒 Explain Like I’m 5 (ELI5 Lede)**
> 1–2 plain-language sentences explaining the concept simply and clearly.  
> _Example:_ “When people drink, their body turns sugar into something that hurts the liver. Turning off that process might stop the damage.”

---

### 3. **Key Findings (3–5 bullets)**
- Summarize mechanisms, evidence, or outcomes.  
- Prioritize cause-and-effect relationships, not quotes.  
- Include major data points or methods if relevant.

---

### 4. **Timeline / How We Got Here**
> A short chronological note showing how this discovery or idea evolved.  
> _Example:_ “Fructose’s role in liver disease was established in the 2010s; new studies now tie it to addiction pathways.”

---

### 5. **State of the Field / Industry**
> Where consensus stands, which theories compete, and what the next frontier looks like.  
> Highlight active researchers, companies, or technologies defining the space.

---

### 6. **Implications / Next Steps**
> What this means in practice — for science, industry, or society.  
> Identify what might happen next (research, funding, regulation, productization).

---

### 7. **💰 Who Will Profit**
> Identify stakeholders who could benefit or be disrupted.  
> Organized by category:
> - **Pharma / Biotech:** Companies with related patents or products.  
> - **Academia / IP:** Labs or universities owning relevant enzyme or pathway IP.  
> - **Investors / Funds:** VCs, corporate ventures, or grants shaping this field.  
> - **Adjacent Markets:** Nutrition, diagnostics, AI health startups, etc.  
> - **Policy / Public:** Regulators or public health programs impacted.

---

### 8. **Sources & Further Reading**
> - Link to original source  
> - 2–3 corroborating or anchor studies  
> - Publication dates or journals for recency context

---

## 🧠 Tone & Length
- Neutral, analytical, and concise.  
- Use declarative, active sentences; skip filler words.  
- Keep total length ~250–400 words (expandable if `--meta` or `--profit` enabled).  
- Avoid overhyping; highlight evidence strength and uncertainties.

---

## ✅ Example Flow (compressed)
**Headline:** Scientists find gut microbes influence sleep.  
**ELI5:** Tiny gut bugs tell your brain when it’s bedtime.  
**Findings:** Gut bacteria make molecules like GABA that affect the brain; sleep issues arise when microbes are imbalanced.  
**Timeline:** Since 2018, studies link microbiome to circadian rhythm.  
**State:** Microbiome-sleep science entering human-trial phase.  
**Implications:** Opens probiotic therapies for insomnia.  
**Who Will Profit:** Probiotic firms, sleep-tech startups, supplement manufacturers.  
**Sources:** ScienceBlog, Nature Metabolism (2025).

---

**End of Directive**
