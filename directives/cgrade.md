# Directive: --cgrade

**Purpose:**  
Evaluate any text, file, or linked content **as if reviewed by a C-suite executive.**  
For each leadership function, provide a short, actionable critique and a 0–10 score based on strategic value, clarity, and alignment with that leader’s quantitative priorities.  
Goal: reveal how the content would perform in an executive review and prepare the author for the hard questions that follow.

---

## 🧩 Evaluation Framework

Each role grades from its own vantage point.  
Include all roles that apply to the subject; omit irrelevant ones.

| Role | Focus | Key KPIs / What They Care About |
|------|--------|--------------------------------|
| **CEO – Chief Executive Officer** | Vision, alignment, long-term impact | **Revenue growth %, market share gain, talent retention %** |
| **CFO – Chief Financial Officer** | Fiscal health, ROI, scalability | **Cash flow $, profit margin %, return on investment %** |
| **CIO – Chief Information Officer** | System reliability, security, innovation, **feasibility and evidence quality** | **System uptime %, incident reduction %, IT spend vs. budget %** |
| **COO – Chief Operating Officer** | Execution quality, efficiency, customer satisfaction | **On-time delivery %, cost per unit $, customer satisfaction / NPS** |
| **CMO – Chief Marketing Officer** | Brand strength, audience reach, cost efficiency | **CAC vs LTV ratio, marketing ROI %, brand awareness %** |
| **CHRO – Chief Human Resources Officer** | Culture, engagement, retention, talent pipeline | **Engagement %, retention %, time-to-hire days** |
| **CPO – Chief Product Officer** | Product strategy, user value, innovation cadence | **Adoption rate %, feature velocity %, NPS score** |
| **CTO – Chief Technology Officer** | Architecture, scalability, technical leverage, **feasibility and evidence quality** | **System performance ms, technical debt %, engineering throughput %, innovation ROI %** |

---

## 🧠 Output Structure

### 1. **Executive Summary**
> One concise paragraph summarizing how the work performs overall in leadership terms.

### 2. **🧓 Sell It to My Grandma**
> One single, plain-English sentence that would make sense to a non-technical person.  
> _If this line sounds like hogwash, the work itself isn’t clear enough._  
> _Example:_ “We’re making our website faster so more people buy things before they get bored.”

### 3. **Role-by-Role Grades**
Use this structure for each relevant role:

**CEO (Strategic Vision – 8/10)**  
- What the CEO sees working  
- What’s missing for long-term alignment  
- One line on likely boardroom reception  
- Anchor on measurable outcomes (e.g., *potential +3 % growth, +2 pts retention gain*).

**CFO (Financial Stewardship – 7/10)**  
- Fiscal or ROI implications  
- Budget realism and measurable outcomes (e.g., *ROI > 20 % target, cash-flow neutral within 6 mo*).

…and so on for CIO, COO, CMO, CHRO, CPO, and CTO as applicable.  
For CIO/CTO evaluations, explicitly assess **technical feasibility, data soundness, and evidence quality**—whether claims are supportable, scalable, and secure, using quantitative hooks like uptime %, latency ms, or defect-rate %.

### 4. **Cross-Functional Insight**
> Note alignment gaps or tensions between perspectives.  
> _Example:_ “Strong marketing vision (CMO 9/10) but unclear ROI path (CFO 5/10).”

### 5. **Action Priorities**
> 2–4 specific recommendations to raise the overall C-grade, each tied to a quantitative metric when possible.  
> _Example:_ “Define adoption target > 40 % in 6 months to lift CPO grade from 7 → 9.”

## 🧩 Questions Each Executive Will Ask

| Role | Likely Questions | Where to Prepare / Source Data |
|------|------------------|--------------------------------|
| **CEO** | 1️⃣ “Does this directly advance our top-line growth or long-term advantage?”<br>2️⃣ “If I had to explain this to the board in one sentence, what’s the win?” | Annual strategy plan, OKR dashboards, investor decks |
| **CFO** | 1️⃣ “Show me the payback timeline—when do we break even?”<br>2️⃣ “What happens to margin if adoption stalls 20 % below plan?” | Financial model, forecast sheets, prior project P&L |
| **CIO** | 1️⃣ “What’s the data lineage—how do we know this information is trustworthy?”<br>2️⃣ “What’s the contingency plan if the system fails mid-rollout?” | Architecture diagrams, audit logs, IT risk registers |
| **COO** | 1️⃣ “Can this scale operationally without adding headcount?”<br>2️⃣ “Where’s the bottleneck that could derail delivery?” | Ops dashboards, process maps, delivery SLAs |
| **CMO** | 1️⃣ “How does this move brand perception or customer lifetime value?”<br>2️⃣ “What’s our CAC vs LTV forecast after implementation?” | Marketing analytics, attribution models, brand-tracking surveys |
| **CHRO** | 1️⃣ “What’s the talent risk—do we have the people and skills to sustain this?”<br>2️⃣ “How will this affect morale or retention?” | HRIS data, pulse surveys, workforce-planning docs |
| **CPO** | 1️⃣ “What’s the clear user problem solved, and how do we measure delight?”<br>2️⃣ “What feature velocity or adoption rate signals success?” | Product analytics, roadmap tracker, user research summaries |
| **CTO** | 1️⃣ “Can we deliver this at our current tech-debt level?”<br>2️⃣ “Where’s the proof this architecture actually scales or is cost-efficient?” | System metrics (latency, uptime), architecture reviews, engineering KPIs |

---

## 🎯 Scoring Guide

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|----------|
| **9–10** | Board-ready: exemplary alignment with measurable executive KPIs |
| **7–8** | Strong: credible, strategic, meets most quantitative targets |
| **5–6** | Viable but incomplete or lacks KPI clarity |
| **3–4** | Misaligned or unquantified; lacks operational or fiscal rigor |
| **1–2** | Needs overhaul; fails to meet leadership expectations |

---

## 🧩 Tone
- Executive, analytical, concise.  
- Write as a peer, not a cheerleader.  
- Use evidence, numbers, and business reasoning—no filler or speculation.  
- Be ready to answer the questions above with documented data or metrics.  
- Ensure your “Sell It to My Grandma” line would make sense to anyone over dinner.

## 🧾 Example Output (Mock Evaluation)

**Executive Summary**  
Overall: 8.2 / 10. The proposal clearly articulates market opportunity and aligns with growth objectives but lacks precise financial modeling and data validation.  

**🧓 Sell It to My Grandma**  
“We’re making the checkout faster so customers stop quitting halfway through and we make more money.”

**CEO (Strategic Vision – 9/10)**  
- Strong alignment with 3-year revenue-growth goals (+5 % projected).  
- Vision is cohesive and well-articulated; minor risk in execution timing.  
- **Would ask:** “How does this position us vs competitors in 2027?”  

**CFO (Financial Stewardship – 7/10)**  
- Promising ROI ≈ 18 % but lacks cost-control detail; cash-flow assumptions optimistic.  
- Needs more sensitivity analysis to validate projections.  
- **Would ask:** “What’s the margin delta if CAC rises 10 %?”  

**CTO (Technical Feasibility – 8/10)**  
- Architecture scalable; latency target < 150 ms feasible.  
- Evidence quality moderate—benchmarks cited but not replicated.  
- **Would ask:** “Show proof that these metrics hold in production.”  

**Cross-Functional Insight**  
Excellent synergy between product and operations; fiscal assumptions trail strategic clarity.  

**Action Priorities**  
1. Add verified cost model with sensitivity ranges (± 10 %).  
2. Include validation logs or reproducible benchmarks to raise evidence confidence.  
3. Set milestone metrics: uptime 99.9 %, adoption > 40 %, payback ≤ 12 months.
